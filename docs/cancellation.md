# Cancelling Your Subscription

## How to cancel
Open Settings > Billing and select "Cancel plan". Only workspace owners can cancel a subscription.

## What happens after cancelling
You keep access to paid features until the end of the current billing period. After that, the workspace moves to the Free plan.

## Your data after cancelling
Your data is kept for 30 days after the workspace is closed. After 30 days it is permanently deleted.
