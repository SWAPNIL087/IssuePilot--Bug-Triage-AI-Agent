# Notifications

## Email notifications
Open Settings > Notifications to choose which events send you an email: assignments, comments, mentions and due-date reminders.

## Daily digest
Turn on "Daily digest" to receive one summary email at 8:00 AM in your time zone instead of individual emails.

## Not receiving emails
Check your spam folder and confirm that notifications are enabled for the event. Add notifications@taskly.example to your contacts to avoid filtering.
