# Plans and Pricing

## Available plans
- Free: $0, up to 5 users, 3 projects.
- Pro: $12 per user per month, or $10 per user per month when billed annually. Unlimited projects and integrations.
- Business: $24 per user per month, or $20 per user per month when billed annually. Adds advanced permissions and priority support.

## Upgrading or downgrading
Open Settings > Billing > Change plan. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Free trial
Pro and Business include a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start the trial.
