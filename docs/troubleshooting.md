# Troubleshooting

## Supported browsers
Taskly works on the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge.

## The page will not load or looks broken
Refresh the page, then clear your browser cache and cookies. If the problem continues, try a private window or a different supported browser.

## Mobile app is not syncing
Make sure you are on the latest version of the app and connected to the internet. Sign out and sign back in to force a full sync.

## Checking service status
Current incidents are posted on status.taskly.example.
