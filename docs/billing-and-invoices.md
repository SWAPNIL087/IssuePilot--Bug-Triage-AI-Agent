# Billing and Invoices

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Update your card under Settings > Billing > Payment method.

## Downloading invoices
Open Settings > Billing > Invoices and select the download icon next to any invoice to get a PDF. Only workspace owners and billing admins can see invoices.

## When you are billed
You are billed on the same day each month (or year) as your original purchase. The invoice is emailed to the billing contact.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails we retry it 3 times over 7 days and email the billing contact each time. If all retries fail, the workspace is moved to the Free plan.

## Changing the billing email
Open Settings > Billing > Billing contact and enter the new email address.
