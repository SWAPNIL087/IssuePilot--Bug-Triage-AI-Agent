# Two-Factor Authentication

## Turning on 2FA
Open Settings > Security and select "Enable two-factor authentication". Scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or Authy, then enter the 6-digit code to confirm.

## Backup codes
When you enable 2FA we show 10 one-time backup codes. Store them somewhere safe. Each code can be used once if you lose access to your authenticator app.

## Lost your device
Sign in with a backup code, then set up 2FA again under Settings > Security. If you have no backup codes, our support team must verify your identity before they can disable 2FA on your account.
