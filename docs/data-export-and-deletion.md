# Data Export and Deletion

## Exporting your data
Workspace owners can export all projects and tasks from Settings > Workspace > Export. Choose CSV or JSON. The export file is emailed to the owner when it is ready, usually within 1 hour.

## Deleting your account
Open Settings > Profile > Delete account. Deleting your account removes your personal data. Workspace content you created stays in the workspace unless the owner deletes the workspace.

## Privacy requests
For other privacy requests, such as access to or correction of your personal data, email privacy@taskly.example. We respond within 30 days.
