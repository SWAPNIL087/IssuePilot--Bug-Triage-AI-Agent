# Refund Policy

## Eligibility
- New monthly or annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of the first purchase.
- Annual renewals can be refunded in full within 7 days of the renewal date.
- Monthly renewals are not refundable.

## What is not refundable
Partial months, unused seats and upgrades or add-ons after the refund window has passed are not refundable.

## How refunds are paid
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## Requesting a refund
To request a refund, contact support within the eligible period. Requests outside these windows are reviewed individually by our billing team, and we cannot promise an outcome.
