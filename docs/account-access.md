# Account Access

## Signing in
Go to app.taskly.example and enter your work email and password. You can also choose "Continue with Google".

## Resetting your password
Select "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter your account email. We send a reset link that is valid for 60 minutes. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder and make sure you used the email address of your Taskly account.

## Locked accounts
After 5 failed sign-in attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait 15 minutes and try again, or reset your password to unlock it right away.

## Changing your email address
Open Settings > Profile, enter the new email address and confirm it using the link we send to the new address.
