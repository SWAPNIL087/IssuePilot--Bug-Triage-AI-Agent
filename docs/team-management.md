# Team Management

## Inviting teammates
Open Settings > Members, select "Invite" and enter their email addresses. Invitations expire after 7 days.

## Roles
- Admin: manages members, billing and settings.
- Member: creates and edits projects and tasks.
- Guest: can only see projects they are invited to.

## Removing a member
Open Settings > Members, select the member and choose "Remove". Their tasks stay in the workspace and become unassigned.

## Seats and billing
You are billed per active member. When you add a member mid-period the charge is prorated. Guests are free.
