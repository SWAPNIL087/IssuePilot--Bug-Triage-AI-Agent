# Orders and Status

## Checking your order status
Send us your order number (it looks like ORD-123456) from the email address you used to place the order. We reply with the current status of that order.

## What the statuses mean
- Pending: we have received the order.
- Invoiced: an invoice has been issued for the order.
- Completed: the order is finished.

## Changing an order's status
Order statuses are updated by our team as the order progresses. If you think the status of your order is wrong, contact support with the order number and we will review it.

## Order numbers
Order numbers have the format ORD- followed by 6 digits. If you cannot find your order number, check your order confirmation email.
